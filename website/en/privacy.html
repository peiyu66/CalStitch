CalStitch Privacy Policy

Which versions this covers: The first App Store release, 1.0 (build 18), does not support iCloud rule sync and stores rules only on your device. The CloudKit rule sync, birthday matching codes, and sync safeguards for event cleanup described below apply only to 1.0.1 (build 36) and later. In all versions, calendar events sync according to your chosen calendar provider and account settings.

This policy explains how CalStitch handles your data. CalStitch does not require an account, send contacts, calendar content, or rules to the developer, or use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Calendar

When you connect a calendar, CalStitch requests full access so it can list destination calendars, match events, and preview, update, or remove the events it manages. It reads information such as dates and notes to identify CalStitch markers. The cleanup tool checks writable calendars within a defined date range. Preview and search do not write events; applying changes or confirming removal does.

Contacts

The system contact picker gives CalStitch access only to the people you select. Group import needs full Contacts access to list groups and read names, nicknames, and birthdays for the selected members. Birthday event titles use nicknames by default, or names when no nickname is available. You can choose to use names instead. CalStitch does not edit your contacts or upload contact identifiers. Any identifiers saved by older versions remain on the device.

For new imports, the app derives a birthday matching code on the device from the name, nickname, calendar type, month, day, leap-month status, and year if supplied. The code is used only to prevent duplicate imports. When rule sync is active, CalStitch syncs this code without uploading an extra copy of the original name, nickname, or birth year. The code does not contain a directly readable name or birthday. It cannot identify a contact on its own.

Storage and sync

Without an iCloud sign-in, rules are stored on your device. When you use iCloud, CalStitch syncs rules, category order, the matching and operation records needed to maintain events, and birthday matching codes to your own private iCloud database. CalStitch does not send these records to its developer, analytics or advertising services, or a public database.

Applying a rule writes events to the system calendar you choose. iCloud, Google, or another calendar provider syncs those events according to your account settings. People with access to a shared calendar may see event titles and notes, including names or nicknames used in birthday event titles.

Permissions and deletion

You can revoke Calendar or Contacts access in your device settings. Doing so does not automatically delete rules or events. You can remove rules and matching events that have not started in CalStitch. Removing a rule also removes its birthday matching code. Sync keeps the rule deletion marker so that an older device cannot bring back a deleted rule. Recent event removal details are used for two hours, then cleared during a sync check.

Uninstalling CalStitch does not delete your private iCloud records or system calendar events. To remove them, delete the rules in CalStitch before uninstalling, and check the original calendar for any events you want to remove. The developer does not retain a copy to delete for you.

Contact
