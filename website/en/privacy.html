CalStitch Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current CalStitch test build. CalStitch does not require an account, send contacts, calendar content, or rules to the developer, or use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Calendar

When you connect a calendar, CalStitch requests full access so it can list destination calendars, match events, and preview, update, or remove the events it manages. It reads information such as dates and notes to identify CalStitch markers. The cleanup tool checks writable calendars within a defined date range. Preview and search do not write events; applying changes or confirming removal does.

Contacts

When you pick individuals in the iPhone contact picker, CalStitch receives only the contacts you select for that import. Group import needs full access to list groups and read names and birthdays from the selected group. CalStitch retains names used by imported rules, birthday details, and contact identifiers to schedule events and detect duplicate imports. It does not edit your contacts.

Storage and sync

Rules, settings, and event matching records are stored in the app on your device. Applying a rule writes the events to the system calendar you selected. If that calendar syncs through iCloud, Google, or another account, the service syncs events according to its settings. Anyone with access to a shared calendar may see event titles and notes, including names used for imported birthdays.

Permissions and deletion

You can revoke access in iPhone Settings. That does not automatically remove rules or events already written. In CalStitch you can remove rules and matching events that have not started. Uninstalling CalStitch does not remove events from your calendar; clean them up first in CalStitch or delete them directly in your calendar.

Contact

For privacy questions, see Support and Contact. GitHub Pages hosts this website. Website visits and GitHub Issues are also subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement.
