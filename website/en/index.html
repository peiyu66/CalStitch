CalStitch

Set up rules in CalStitch and see the events in the calendar you already use.

Schedule lunar birthdays, solar terms, or something as simple as an appointment every other week. Organize rules into categories and preview the events before you apply changes.

Add only the holidays, anniversaries, and contacts’ birthdays you want to your own calendar. There’s no need to subscribe to a full holiday calendar or show every contact’s birthday.

Get started

The app is available in Chinese and English. CalStitch is not yet on the App Store.
